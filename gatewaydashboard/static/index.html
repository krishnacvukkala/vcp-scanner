🔄 The 5-Step Workflow — How Aaroquant Works

Every trade you make follows a disciplined, 5-step process. The top bar of the dashboard always shows where you are in this journey.

01 SCAN
→
02 ANALYZE
→
03 RESEARCH
→
04 RISK
→
05 PLAN
1

SCAN — Find the Best Opportunities

The AI scans 160+ global stocks across India (NSE), USA (NYSE/NASDAQ), UK (LSE), Germany (XETRA), and more. It looks for the "Aaroquant Swing Trades" pattern — a specific price contraction structure that historically precedes strong breakouts. Think of it as the AI doing all the chart-reading homework for you.

2

ANALYZE — Technical Analysis

For each candidate found in Step 1, the system runs deep technical analysis: checking the base structure, how tight the price contractions are, volume patterns, distance to the breakout pivot point, and relative strength. Only stocks that pass all technical gates move forward.

3

RESEARCH — Fundamentals, News & Catalysts

For the technically qualified stocks, the system pulls real financial data: quarterly revenue, earnings per share (EPS), market cap, P/E ratio, 52-week range, recent news, and AI-generated research summaries. A stock with strong technicals AND strong fundamentals is a much higher-probability trade.

4

RISK — Calculate Risk & Position Size

Before entering any trade, the system calculates exactly how much you could lose (Stop Loss), how much you could gain (Take Profit 1 and 2), the Risk:Reward ratio, and the recommended position size based on your account. This is arguably the most important step — never trade without knowing your risk first.

5

PLAN — Generate a Complete Trade Plan

Once you've completed all 4 steps, the system generates a complete, actionable trade plan: Entry Price, Stop Loss, Take Profit 1 & 2, Position Size, Account Risk %, Time Horizon, and Win Probability. This plan is ready to execute — no guesswork needed.

💡 Pro Tip: Never skip steps! A stock with a great chart but terrible fundamentals, or a great setup with no risk plan, is not a complete trade idea.

📊 Chart & Analysis

This is your detailed candlestick chart with technical overlays. It's the most powerful visual tool in the dashboard for reading price action.

How to Read Candlestick Charts
Each "candle" represents one time period (1 day, 1 week, etc.) and shows 4 prices: Open, High, Low, Close (OHLC).

🟢 Green candle = price closed HIGHER than it opened (buyers won that day)
🔴 Red candle = price closed LOWER than it opened (sellers won that day)
The thin lines (wicks) show the highest and lowest prices touched during that period.

📐 The Base Structure Panel (below the chart)

Below the main chart you'll see a breakdown table showing each "contraction wave" detected by the AI. Each row represents one price swing:

ColumnMeaning
Wave #The contraction number (Wave 1 = widest, last wave = tightest)
Depth %How much the stock pulled back during that wave. Smaller = tighter = more bullish
DurationHow many days the wave lasted

✅ What to look for: Each wave should be smaller than the previous one (e.g. 18% → 12% → 7% → 4%). This "tightening" pattern is the core of the Aaroquant Swing Trades strategy. The tighter the last wave, the closer to a potential breakout.

📖 Full Glossary of Terms

All the trading and strategy terms used in this dashboard — explained in plain English.

Aaroquant Swing TradesThe proprietary trading strategy used in this dashboard. It looks for stocks in a strong uptrend that are "tightening" into a base pattern before their next big move up.
BaseA period of sideways consolidation after a strong uptrend. The stock "rests" before its next move. Think of it as a spring coiling before being released.
Base FormingThe stock has started building its base but hasn't completed it. Not actionable yet — add to watchlist and wait for "Watch" or "Valid Setup" status.
BreakoutWhen a stock's price moves ABOVE the pivot point (resistance level) on high volume. This is the buy signal — the spring has been released.
ContractionEach individual pullback wave within the base. Healthy contractions get progressively smaller (e.g. 18% → 12% → 7%). Each contraction shows the stock is tightening.
Distance to PivotHow far the current price is from the breakout level. -5% means the stock needs to rise 5% to trigger a breakout. +1% means it has already broken out.
Entry PriceThe specific price the AI recommends you buy at. Usually just above the pivot to confirm the breakout is real before committing.
EPSEarnings Per Share. The company's total profit divided by all its shares. Growing EPS = the company is getting more profitable.
Market CapMarket Capitalisation. The total market value of all shares = share price × number of shares. "£66B" = 66 billion pounds. Large cap = more stable.
Moving Average (MA)The average price over a set number of days. MA50 = average of last 50 days. A stock above its MA50 and MA200 is in a healthy uptrend.
Net IncomeThe company's actual profit after all expenses, taxes, etc. are paid. This is the "bottom line" — what the company actually keeps.
P/E RatioPrice-to-Earnings Ratio. How many times the annual earnings you're paying. P/E of 10 = you pay 10× the annual profit. Lower is generally "cheaper".
Pivot PointThe resistance price level at the top of the base. When the stock breaks above this level on volume, it triggers the buy signal. The most important price level on the chart.
Position SizeThe number of shares to buy. Calculated so that if the Stop Loss is hit, you lose no more than 1% of your total account. This keeps any single loss manageable.
Revenue (TTM)Total sales for the last 12 months (Trailing Twelve Months). This is the company's gross income before expenses.
Risk:Reward RatioCompares potential profit to potential loss. 2.4:1 means for each ₹1 risked, you can make ₹2.40. The higher this number, the better the trade mathematically.
ROEReturn on Equity. How efficiently the company uses shareholders' money to generate profit. ROE above 15% is generally considered strong.
Stage 2The "mark-up" phase in Stan Weinstein's model — when a stock is in a clear uptrend above its 200-day moving average. Aaroquant Swing Trades only operates in Stage 2 stocks.
Stop LossA pre-defined price where you MUST sell if the trade goes wrong. It limits your loss to a fixed, acceptable amount. NEVER trade without a stop loss.
Take ProfitPre-defined price targets where you sell to lock in gains. TP1 = first exit (2R), TP2 = second/final exit (3R). Having targets stops you from being greedy and giving profits back.
Valid SetupThe highest confidence signal — stock has passed ALL technical AND fundamental checks. The buy trigger is active. This is an actionable trade today.
Watch Near PivotStock is almost ready — within 3-5% of its pivot. Not quite at the buy trigger yet. Watch it daily and be ready to act when it breaks out.
Win RateWhat % of past setups were profitable based on backtesting. 73% means 73 out of every 100 historical setups made money.
VolumeThe number of shares traded. HIGH volume on an up-day = strong conviction. LOW volume on a down-day = healthy pullback. Volume confirms price moves.
